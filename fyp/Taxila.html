About Taxila

Taxila is one of the most important archaeological sites in Asia, located in Punjab, Pakistan. Founded around 1000 BCE, it became a major Buddhist learning center and has ruins spanning several centuries. The city was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1980 and attracts visitors from around the world.

Famous Places in Taxila

Explore the ancient heritage and historical landmarks

🏛️

Taxila Museum

One of the most important museums in Pakistan, housing over 4,000 artifacts from the Gandhara civilization including Buddhist sculptures, jewelry, and ancient coins.

Museum UNESCO History
⛩️

Dharmarajika Stupa

The largest and most impressive stupa in Taxila, dating back to the 3rd century BCE. Built by Emperor Ashoka to house Buddhist relics.

Buddhist Site Ancient
🏰

Sirkap City Ruins

Ancient city ruins from the 2nd century BCE, showcasing Greek, Persian, and Indian architectural influences. Features the famous Double-Headed Eagle stupa.

Archaeological Ruins
🗿

Jaulian Monastery

Well-preserved Buddhist monastery complex with stunning stucco sculptures and elaborate stone carvings, offering panoramic views of Taxila valley.

Monastery Buddhist
⛰️

Mohra Muradu

Buddhist monastery complex from the 2nd century CE, featuring a beautiful main stupa surrounded by chapels containing Buddha statues.

Monastery Scenic
🏺

Bhir Mound

The oldest settlement in Taxila, dating back to the 6th century BCE. Contains ruins from the Achaemenid and Mauryan periods.

Ancient City Historical
🕌

Jandial Temple

A Zoroastrian fire temple with Greek architectural influence, one of the few remaining structures of its kind in Pakistan.

Temple Unique
🏛️

Sirsukh Ruins

Ancient fortified city built by the Kushans in the 1st century CE, featuring massive defensive walls and urban planning.

Fortification Kushan Era
